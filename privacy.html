GameZone & Vault Shelf Privacy Policy

Transparency and user control are at the core of our parlor and personal media shelf. Here is our pledge on how your data is handled on our platform.

📅 Last Updated: October 2026 🔒 Client-Side Storage 🚫 Zero Tracking Cookies 💾 Full JSON Data Portability
💻

100% Client-Side Storage

Your media collections, custom reviews, ratings, and gaming backlog remain exclusively stored inside your local browser.

🚫

Zero Behavioral Ads

We do not run advertising pixels, third-party trackers, or marketing trackers across any page of this site.

📦

Instant JSON Export

Own every single byte of your data. You can back up and download your entire library in standard JSON format anytime.

⚡

Instant Slot Reservation

Slot bookings on the parlor page are executed client-side for confirmation without storing credit cards or financial telemetry.

01

Scope & Architecture

This Privacy Policy applies to both the GameZone Parlor portal and the Vault & Shelf (My Media Library) application. Our architectural principle is simple: what happens on your device stays on your device.

We do not maintain centralized user databases that harvest your identity, gaming habits, or movie preferences.

02

Information We Store Locally

When using Vault & Shelf, data is saved locally via HTML5 window.localStorage to enable persistent sessions across reloads:

Storage Key Description Storage Mechanism
vault_shelf_games_v2 Games list, user ratings, hours played, and custom notes Browser LocalStorage
vault_shelf_movies_v2 Movies list, watchlist status, ratings, and personal reviews Browser LocalStorage
vault_shelf_user_profile_v1 User avatar selection, nickname, and personal collector tagline Browser LocalStorage
media_poster_cache_v1 Verified cover art caching for fast rendering Browser LocalStorage
03

GameZone Parlor Slot Reservations

The reservation form on the GameZone Parlor landing page is designed for convenient slot scheduling:

  • No payment credentials, credit card numbers, or bank tokens are ever requested or stored on this web page.
  • Slot selections are confirmed interactively in the browser view without sending tracking beacons to third-party ad networks.
04

Third-Party Media Assets & External Links

To provide rich artwork and trailers:

  • Game posters and movie covers are fetched from verified public CDNs (such as Wikimedia Commons, TMDB, or game publisher promotional kits).
  • Clicking on YouTube trailer links redirects to YouTube where Google's Terms and Privacy Notice apply.
  • External typography is served via Google Fonts CDN under their respective privacy policies.
05

Your Rights & Data Erasure

Under standard data protection frameworks (including GDPR, CCPA, and global privacy principles), you hold full autonomous control:

  • Right to Access & Portability: Export your complete collection as a portable JSON file from the dashboard or privacy page.
  • Right to Rectification: Edit details, cover art, and playtime for any game or movie instantly.
  • Right to Erasure: Wipe your local storage at any time by selecting the "Reset to Default" feature or clearing your browser cache.
