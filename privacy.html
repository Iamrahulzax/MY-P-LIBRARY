Security & Privacy Architecture

GameZone & Vault Shelf Privacy Policy

Transparency and client-side control are the foundation of this platform. Review our architecture and verifiable data handling practices below.

Updated: October 2026 Local Browser Storage Zero Tracking Cookies Full JSON Portability

Client-Side Storage

Media collections, custom ratings, hours logged, and notes remain inside your local browser storage.

Zero Behavioral Ads

No tracking scripts, third-party analytics pixels, or marketing beacons are embedded in this application.

Direct JSON Export

Maintain complete ownership of your library. Back up, download, and transfer your data in JSON anytime.

Local Reservations

Parlor bookings are saved in your browser session for check-in without collecting payment tokens or sensitive telemetry.

01

Scope & Architecture

This Privacy Policy applies to the GameZone Parlor portal and the Vault & Shelf personal media shelf. Our architectural foundation ensures that your personal records remain stored on your own device rather than harvested on remote centralized marketing databases.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker personal records to data aggregators or advertising networks.

02

Information Stored Locally in Browser Storage

When using this web application, data is maintained locally via HTML5 window.localStorage to preserve records across page reloads:

Storage Key Purpose Mechanism
gamezone_reservations Active parlor seat bookings and scheduling references Local Browser Storage
vault_shelf_games_v2 Tracked games, custom ratings, status, hours, and notes Local Browser Storage
vault_shelf_movies_v2 Tracked movies, watchlists, ratings, and reviews Local Browser Storage
vault_shelf_user_profile_v1 Collector profile handle and avatar preference Local Browser Storage
gamezone_cookie_acknowledged Storage preference banner dismissal status Local Browser Storage
03

Station Bookings & Payment Handling

The reservation form on the GameZone Parlor portal is provided strictly for seat allocation:

  • No payment credentials, credit card numbers, or bank account tokens are ever requested or stored on this website.
  • Reservations are saved locally so you can review reference details and cancel bookings directly from the portal.
04

External APIs & Asset Fetching

To resolve game metadata and poster artwork securely:

  • Our proxy server at /api/proxy shields third-party API keys (TMDB and RAWG) on the server side without exposing credentials to client browsers.
  • Media poster images are rendered from verified external image hosts over HTTPS.
  • Typography assets are loaded from Google Fonts CDN under Google's standard privacy guidelines.
05

User Rights & Data Erasure

In accordance with applicable privacy frameworks (including GDPR, CCPA, and global principles):

  • Right to Portability: You can export your full library in JSON format at any time.
  • Right to Rectification: Edit or correct any logged entry, rating, or review instantaneously.
  • Right to Erasure: Clear your records at any time by selecting the reset function in the admin vault or clearing your browser's site data.
