# 🎮🎬 My Personal Gaming & Movie Library

A personal web app where I keep track of the **games I have played and the movies I have watched**.

This is my own digital collection: a place to remember what I've experienced, what I enjoyed, what I completed, and what I want to play or watch next.

---

## 🧠 Why I Made This

I wanted one place to maintain my personal collection instead of trying to remember everything.

The idea is simple:

> **Every game I play and every movie I watch becomes part of my personal library.**

This project is mainly for **myself**, so the focus is on simplicity, personalization, and keeping a history of my entertainment.

---

# 🎮 Gaming Collection

I use this section to keep track of games I have played.

For each game, I can store:

* 🎮 Game name
* 🖼️ Cover
* 💻 Platform
* 🏷️ Genre
* 📅 Release year
* ⭐ My rating
* 🎯 Status
* ⏱️ Hours played
* 📅 Date played/completed
* ❤️ Favorite
* 📝 Personal notes
* 💭 My review

* 📚 Backlog

```bash

```
