About GameZone Parlor

GameZone Parlor is an in-person gaming space engineered for competitive scrims, co-op multiplayer, and individual sessions. Stations feature calibrated OLED displays, clean mechanical inputs, dedicated fiber connections with sub-15ms regional pings, and ergonomic seating. We sanitize peripherals between each session and provide pre-installed AAA titles, competitive patches, and controller options for all major platforms.
